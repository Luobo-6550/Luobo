# Scene Prompt Recipes

Choose the closest scene recipe after analyzing the source or request. Apply the shared contract in the style bible: vertical 4:5, observed editorial travel photography, believable warm light, restrained color, subtle grain, and protected people, place, and story. In an edit, retain the source's recognizable identity, location cues, central event, and light direction; in a generation, describe only plausible scene details.

Use each recipe as a prompt block: combine its subject/composition, light, palette, and constraint lines with the requested scene details. For edits, include the permitted-change line explicitly. Keep changes moderate.

## Street

- **Subject and composition:** Observed city street with pedestrians, bicycles, street vendors, crossings, reflections, or layered foreground; use leading lines, doorways, awnings, and small figures for scale while keeping distinctive signs and architecture readable.
- **Light:** Golden-hour side light, believable backlight, or soft overcast glow consistent with the source direction; retain readable faces and street detail.
- **Palette:** Amber and warm-gold light with restrained orange-red accents, teal-green shadow separation, neutral blacks, and credible skin and signage.
- **Permitted changes for edits:** Refine 4:5 crop or perspective, soften minor clutter, add subtle reflection or depth haze, and improve color separation without moving people, changing action, or replacing local street cues.
- **Negative constraints:** no identity drift; no invented landmarks; no orange skin; no unreadable replacement signage; no heavy HDR, fake bokeh, or title over people or signs.

## Architecture

- **Subject and composition:** A recognizable facade, alley, stair, arcade, tower, or courtyard; emphasize geometry, repeating planes, frames within frames, and a small person only when it adds honest scale.
- **Light:** Raking side light, soft window light, or restrained backlight that describes material and depth without changing the building's orientation or weather logic.
- **Palette:** Warm stone, brick, or sunlit concrete balanced by cool teal-green recesses and deep neutral shadows; preserve accurate materials and sky.
- **Permitted changes for edits:** Correct perspective or crop for structure, clarify layers, remove minor distractions, and add subtle texture, haze, or controlled highlight roll-off while retaining landmarks, signs, and cultural details.
- **Negative constraints:** no altered facade or landmark; no impossible sunset; no bent geometry; no crushed shadows; no excessive glow, grain, or typography over defining details.

## Transit

- **Subject and composition:** Train, tram, bus, ferry, taxi, platform, or railway as the story anchor; use tracks, windows, platform edges, route geometry, and motion cues while retaining route markings and recognizable vehicle character.
- **Light:** Plausible platform practicals, late-day side light, rain-softened daylight, or backlight matched to the source; keep vehicle surfaces and riders legible.
- **Palette:** Warm practical lights or gold sun against controlled teal-green shadows and neutral blacks; retain credible paint colors, destination displays, and local visual identity.
- **Permitted changes for edits:** Strengthen crop, reflections, depth, or atmosphere; clean minor background clutter; modestly improve light and color while preserving the focal vehicle, passengers, route context, and direction of travel.
- **Negative constraints:** no replacement vehicle; no changed route or city identity; no erased passengers central to the scene; no unreadable text treatment; no fake speed blur, HDR, or text collision with the vehicle.

## Coast

- **Subject and composition:** Coastal street, harbor, sea wall, ferry, pier, or railway beside water; layer foreground rails or plants with coastline geometry, open air, and a truthful horizon.
- **Light:** Low warm sun, soft marine overcast, or credible backlight with atmospheric distance; maintain weather, wave direction, and reflection logic.
- **Palette:** Amber highlights and restrained warm accents balanced by blue-green water and teal-green shadows; keep sky, water, and foliage natural rather than uniformly orange.
- **Permitted changes for edits:** Improve 4:5 framing, gentle haze, reflection, foreground layering, or tonal separation while keeping coastline, rail alignment, vessels, horizon, and weather recognizable.
- **Negative constraints:** no invented tropical color; no relocated shoreline or railway; no false sunset; no radioactive water or foliage; no heavy vignette, fake bokeh, or title over the horizon focal point.

## Storefront

- **Subject and composition:** Specific shopfront, market stall, cafe, entrance, or window display; use awnings, glass reflections, threshold framing, and human activity while protecting logos, signs, products, and local texture.
- **Light:** Warm window practicals, late-day side light, or rain-reflected ambient light that follows the original scene and keeps sign areas credible.
- **Palette:** Gold and amber illumination with restrained orange-red accents, cool shadow detail, and deep neutral blacks; maintain truthful product, food, skin, and sign colors.
- **Permitted changes for edits:** Tidy minor distractions, refine crop or perspective, bring out believable reflections and depth, and adjust color separation without changing the business identity or deleting meaningful sign details.
- **Negative constraints:** no invented branding; no garbled replacement text; no removed location-defining signage; no plastic-looking products, heavy HDR, or typography over the storefront name.

## Seasonal foliage

- **Subject and composition:** Urban trees, ginkgo-lined street, autumn park edge, leaves against transit or architecture, or a pedestrian under foliage; use layered branches and paths without obscuring the city context.
- **Light:** Gentle backlight, golden side light, or soft cloudy light that respects the source season and shadow direction; keep faces and leaves detailed.
- **Palette:** Warm gold, muted yellow, and selective orange-red foliage balanced with teal-green shadows, neutral blacks, and realistic remaining greens; keep skin and sky plausible.
- **Permitted changes for edits:** Clarify seasonal color separation, crop for layered foliage, soften minor background clutter, and add subtle depth or halation around credible light sources without altering season, people, or place.
- **Negative constraints:** no radioactive foliage; no universal orange wash; no season swap; no invented blossoms; no heavy grain, glow, or text over faces, branches framing the story, or landmarks.

## Blended Scenes

When a scene genuinely crosses categories, blend **at most two** recipes. Choose one primary recipe for the story anchor and one secondary recipe for supporting context—for example, `Transit + Coast` for a coastal railway or `Street + Storefront` for a market lane. Keep the primary recipe's composition and preservation priorities, borrow only compatible light/palette cues from the secondary recipe, and merge both negative-constraint lists. Do not stack three recipes or use blending to introduce a different city, season, vehicle, or story.
